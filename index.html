About Oyindamola

A voice of substance,
faith & purpose.

Oyindamola Babatunde is a Canadian-based community architect, keynote speaker, and lifestyle catalyst committed to reshaping how modern women live, lead, and steward their homes.

Like so many women, Oyinda experienced firsthand the quiet overwhelm of modern life while juggling marriage, raising little ones, managing career transitions, and trying to build financial security in a world that constantly tells mothers they have to do it all on their own. Rather than accepting exhaustion and loneliness as the norm, she decided to build what she wished existed: spaces where women can breathe, grow financially, strengthen their faith, and truly support one another.

She is the founder of the Busy Mamas Network, leading an active community of 500+ mothers across Canada and beyond, and the visionary behind Busy Mamas Consulting, delivering strategic advisory and personal guidance for women building purposeful, thriving, and financially sound lives, alongside practical tools like the Busy Mamas Card Game.

“I believe motherhood is not an interruption to purpose. It is one of the highest arenas of leadership on earth.”
